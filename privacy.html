Thread Fight — Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Thread Fight is a browser extension that turns a comment-section argument on Facebook, LinkedIn or Reddit into an animated fight video refereed by an AI model that you choose. This policy explains what the extension reads, where it sends it, and what it keeps.

What Thread Fight reads

  • Only when you click the Thread Fight toolbar button on a Facebook, LinkedIn or Reddit page, and only on that tab.
  • The text of the comments and replies that are currently visible on the post you select, the commenters' display names, which comment each reply answers, the post's text, and the page address. It does not open, expand or click anything on the page.
  • If no comment thread can be recognized and you choose “Use visible page text”, the visible text of the page's main column instead.

Where it goes

  • Before anything leaves your browser, commenter names and usernames (and mentions of them in comment text) are replaced with “User 1”, “User 2” and so on.
  • The anonymized text is sent over HTTPS (or to a server on your own computer or network) to the single AI provider you configure in Settings: Anthropic, OpenAI, or a server address you enter. That provider's own privacy policy and data terms apply to what it receives.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer of Thread Fight. Thread Fight has no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

  • Settings and API keys are kept in your browser's extension storage on your computer, until you change them or remove the extension.
  • The captured thread and the generated fight script are kept in the browser's session storage so the result tab can work. They are erased when the browser closes.
  • Videos and script files you download are saved by your browser to your computer like any other download.
  • The date you accepted the in-extension disclosure is stored so it isn't shown again.

What Thread Fight does not do

  • It does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone other than the AI provider you choose, and only to referee the thread you selected.
  • It does not use data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness or lending, or any purpose unrelated to making the fight video.
  • It does not read pages you haven't clicked the toolbar button on, or collect browsing history.

Your choices

  • Use a local AI server so that no comment text leaves your network.
  • Remove the extension at any time from chrome://extensions. This deletes its stored settings and keys.
